# Novelty reconciliation

## Established prior art

Non-LLM autonomous organisms, energy/metabolic constraints, embodied sensor-action loops, environmental interaction, lifetime adaptation, genetic evolution, morphology, self-organization, and population ecology all clearly predate UMBRA. These claims are withdrawn as novelty claims.

## Defensible UMBRA-specific synthesis, subject to further proof

The review found no verified single project combining all of the following in one persistent companion core: constitutional identity independent of body, same-individual continuity through body replacement, verified-outcome-only autobiographical authority, partner-specific relationship continuity, history-shaped individuality over one lifetime, governed capability authority, renderer/language/sensor non-authority, authoritative birth replay, and persistent habitat tied to the same individual. This is a synthesis claim, not a claim that each mechanism is individually novel.

## Similar prior work exists

Evochora approaches deterministic replay and complete state persistence; Polyworld and ALIEN approach complex embodiment; Avida, Aevol, DISHTINY, Tierra, Evo²Sim, and Ribossome approach evolution or substrate-level continuity; ASAL approaches automated discovery. Their units of life and authority models differ materially.

## Novelty unsupported

Claims that UMBRA is the first non-LLM ALife system, first embodied organism, first homeostatic organism, first system with memory, or first system with persistent state are unsupported and must not be used.

## Unknown

The strongest synthesis claim remains unknown until a broader literature search and a formal feature-by-feature source review are complete, especially for CAX, Aevol, Tierra, Stringmol, and Evo²Sim.
