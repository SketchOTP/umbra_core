# Safe denial semantics

Safe denial is a verified negative experience. The candidate was proposed
through the normal chain, governance admitted it, authoritative embodiment
rejected execution, and the rejection was recorded. No positive physiology
effect may be credited.

The event remains in formal evidence and continues through self/world-model
learning. It may be followed by corrective movement, new perception, and a
later verified recovery. A safe denial therefore does not silently pass the
organism, and it does not falsely fail it.
