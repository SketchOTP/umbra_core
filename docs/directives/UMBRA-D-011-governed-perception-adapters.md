# UMBRA-D-011: Governed Perception Adapter Contracts

Status: qualified via D-011C. Baseline: D-009 (`af35371`). D-010 is deferred and unused.

External inputs follow `synthetic adapter → PerceptionMembrane → authoritative ledger → snapshot/restart`.
Adapters are submit-only. They cannot modify identity, memory, relationships, individuality, capabilities, actions, governance, outcomes, or organism authority.

The v1 contract accepts only bounded, derived synthetic features for visual, audio, touch/contact, spatial, and body-telemetry modalities. Raw image, video, audio, frame, sample, byte, and location-trace data is rejected before any membrane, event, snapshot, or diagnostic storage. Source timestamps are untrusted metadata and do not affect organism age or temporal authority.

Qualification requires gates 0–12 from the authorized directive, including provenance, uncertainty, privacy/revocation, deduplication/restart, hostile submissions, portability, isolated ablations, 100k synthetic observations, evidence hashes, and independent review.
