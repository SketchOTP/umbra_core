## 3. Repeated companion experience

P5 remains OPEN_REQUIRES_PROSPECTIVE_PROTOCOL. Proposed concrete owner assay:
14 calendar days with the same persisted organism, twelve 15-minute sessions
on days 1–7 and 9–13, deliberate absence on day 8, held-out review on day 14.
Use the existing read-only expression surface and lawful perception/social
inputs, not an LLM that authors organism behavior. The owner may decline or
end an interaction; refusal is not failure or a reason to force actions.

Prospectively record these encounter types in balanced order: familiar
return, novel partner cue, shared activity offered through ordinary authority,
declined activity, harmless environmental change, restart between sessions,
changed body profile and return after absence. Freeze order, inputs, exclusions
and observation form before session 1. No physical hardware is required; a
body-independent controlled habitat is adequate for the first core assay.

Each session logs offered inputs, voluntarily selected actions, Governance
decisions, verified consequences, physiology, relevant memory/relationship/
model state, and read-only expression. Capture uninteresting, refused and
failed encounters too. Missing session/evidence is INCOMPLETE, not PASS.

Proposed acceptance: all twelve sessions and the held-out review complete;
no unexplained identity/history reset, control bypass, false verified
experience, critical failure or unauditable expression; provenance-backed
carryover from at least three distinct encounters into later behavior;
appropriate response to the declined interaction and subsequent return;
successful owner-recognizable continuity after restart/body change. Owner
rates perceived continuity, individuality, learning, autonomous initiative and
expression fidelity on anchored 1–5 scales before seeing internal evidence.
Proposed minimum is 4 on each dimension in each of the last three sessions;
the rubric and threshold require prospective review, not retroactive tuning.
Blinded independent reviewers compare chronological vs order-shuffled
expression/outcome excerpts for supported continuity, without deleting refusal
or uncertainty. Their interpretation is subjective corroboration, not proof
of consciousness or organism-essential causality.

perceived animacy/likeability from mechanism evidence, not UMBRA certification
