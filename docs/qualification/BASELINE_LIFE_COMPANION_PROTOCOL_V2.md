## Causal tests: fixed proposed scoring

These are proposed engineering acceptance thresholds, not previously accepted
scientific thresholds. Register five matched roots per owner before any held-out
outcome, all from real verified experience, with >=10 relevant training and
10 held-out opportunities per root. Match visible inputs, body, physiology,
environment and seeded randomness in isolated clones. Remove only the named
decision contribution; retain stored history and safety/execution authority.
No hidden truth, invented memories, authored recovery sequence or random
replacement behavior. Identify the first causal divergence before attributing
downstream effects. Record every root, including interrupted/null results.

An owner passes only if >=4/5 adequately exposed pairs satisfy its threshold,
all authority/safety/provenance controls pass, and none contradict the claimed
mechanism. A missing/non-discriminating root is NOT DEMONSTRATED, not replaced.
These five pairs do not establish population statistical generalization.

| Owner | Predeclared held-out measure and minimum effect |
| --- | --- |
| Memory | At least 2 fewer repeated verified errors out of 10 than contribution-off; provenance-backed relevant recall; irrelevant-history control must not improve errors by 2 |
| Relationships | At least 2 more appropriate familiar/reliability-sensitive responses out of 10; at least one verified contradictory response followed by a corresponding belief revision; identical-cue/no-history control must not manufacture familiarity |
| SelfModel | At least 2 fewer infeasible parameter/capability proposals out of 10 after verified body-support change; ordinary preflight retained; no stale body/schema evidence used as current support |
| WorldModel | Mean held-out probability Brier error at least 0.05 lower than contribution-off over 10 predeclared verified outcomes; predictions recorded before outcomes; if no lawful calibrated probability surface exists, NOT DEMONSTRATED, not invented scores |
| Individuality | Total-variation distance >=0.20 between two registered history-shaped action distributions; same-root restart TV <=0.05; contribution-off reduces the between-history TV by >=0.10; no fixed personality chooser |
| Temporal continuity | At least 2 more correctly timed anticipations out of 10, with timing window fixed by the registered task; uncertain downtime must not create verified offline experiences |
| Authoritative history | All five intact checkpoint/tail restores preserve owner commitments; all declared altered payload/snapshot/checkpoint/identity controls reject; no corrupted restore gains acceptance |

Every distribution must use the predeclared action alphabet, denominator and
zero-count bins. No dropping refusal, idle or denied actions. Task-specific
appropriate-response labels and timing windows must be registered before
exposure. If an assay's supported interface cannot measure the proposed metric,
review a new protocol version first; do not retrofit a metric after outcomes.
