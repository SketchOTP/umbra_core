# End Goal Statement

Build the persistent organism and brain core for a believable autonomous digital pet companion — comparable in experience to owning a living Digimon or Pokémon.

UMBRA is the persistent internal life of the companion. The avatar, robot, dialogue model, animations, and sensory hardware are bodies and interfaces around that life. The objective is not to simulate every molecular mechanism of biology. The objective is to build the strongest technically defensible artificial creature core capable of becoming a persistent, developing, autonomous, individually recognizable companion.

## Primary target — companion organism core

The core must support:

* persistent identity
* continuous autonomous existence
* homeostatic needs and regulation
* internally generated action
* learning from consequences
* developmental change
* selective long-term memory
* habits and preferences
* relationship formation
* individual temperament shaped by history
* coherent embodied expression
* transfer between virtual and physical bodies
* optional language that expresses rather than controls the organism

The core must not depend upon:

* an LLM as the central controller
* scripted emotional responses
* predefined personality performances
* user prompts to remain active
* direct commands such as become happy, bond, eat, play, sleep, or become afraid

The mature companion will inhabit a persistent digital environment presented as though the computer screen were glass separating its world from ours. It will perceive activity beyond that boundary through a webcam and microphone, gradually learn recurring people, sounds, patterns, and relationships, and respond through movement, posture, proximity, environmental manipulation, and nonverbal vocalization. Optional language may accompany expression; it must never be the seat of the organism’s will.

Its behavior must remain autonomous when no person is present. It must explore, rest, maintain itself, alter its environment, consolidate experience, form habits, develop preferences, and continue changing over time without waiting for user input.

No central controller may directly command the organism to eat, play, sleep, repair, approach, bond, become afraid, or perform a scripted emotional response. These outcomes must emerge from its internal needs, regulation, body, memory, relationships, and developmental history.

## Success criteria

The companion core will be considered successful when:

1. A single persistent individual continues across sessions, upgrades, and body transfers without being re-authored as a new character.
2. Its continued activity depends on real internal need and regulatory dynamics, not on user prompts or scripted schedules alone.
3. Needs, fatigue, recovery, development, and relationship change occur causally rather than through commanded state flips.
4. Different life histories produce observably different individuals (temperament, habits, preferences, bonds).
5. Past experiences selectively alter future perception, behavior, and relationships.
6. It independently develops coherent behaviors and rituals that were not explicitly authored as personality performances.
7. Its actions are understandable from its condition and history but are not mechanically predictable.
8. It continues living and developing when unobserved.
9. Removing essential regulatory organization or accumulated history causes measurable loss of identity and capability.
10. An observer can reasonably perceive it as a persistent living companion rather than an animated application, chatbot, or game character.

The project does not claim to prove consciousness. Its goal is the strongest technically defensible artificial creature core whose apparent aliveness comes from what it is and how it continues to exist — not from how convincingly it has been scripted to perform life.

## Optional long-range research (non-gating)

Digital chemistry, self-producing protocells, autopoietic membranes, molecular metabolism, and bottom-up cellular reproduction are **not** required foundations for the companion core. They may be investigated later as optional scientific extensions. Artificial-chemistry and protocell systems do **not** gate the core companion architecture and must not be framed as UMBRA-D-000A or as a prerequisite to UMBRA-D-001.
