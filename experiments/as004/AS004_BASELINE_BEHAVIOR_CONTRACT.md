# AS-004 baseline behavior contract

The AS-004-enabled path retains the existing organism authority boundary. Candidate
and proposal sources contribute to one canonical pool; hard/current-state
admissibility is applied before the bounded continuation relation. The continuation
relation is computed from an immutable source-backed root before candidate-specific
assessment and may only remove a candidate when a known continuation is preserved by
one candidate and destroyed by another without an asymmetric `UNKNOWN`.

Residual nondominance remains in the existing distributed competition and
candidate-local stochastic resolution. Exactly one current candidate then passes
through Governance, Embodiment, execution, VerifiedOutcome, and selected-only
learning. Critical recovery remains a separate hard authority.

When `bounded_continuation_enabled` is false, the pre-AS-004 behavior remains
available for baseline comparison. When it is true, the old ordinary WorldModel
plan/bias lane and one-step `option.*` preservation channel are bypassed so they do
not compete with the continuation relation. This flag is the only activation seam;
the default remains false until a separately frozen scientific candidate enables it.

The continuation layer has no execution, persistence, learning, renderer, Habitat
truth, utility, weight, source-priority, or future-action-queue authority.
